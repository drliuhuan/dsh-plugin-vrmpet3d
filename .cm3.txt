The reported band was missing from the origin the page assumes — that is the 鼠标穿透

Operator, 2026-10-13: 「独立桌宠会莫名奇妙鼠标穿透，右键点击后菜单点击没反应」. Root cause found by reading the two halves against
each other, and made permanent by my own band change an hour earlier.

`desktop/main.js` sizes and places the window on `petRect ∪ menuRect ∪ statusRect ∪ speechRect` — the rects the page
REPORTS. The page, meanwhile, laid everything out against `plannedOrigin()`, which unioned only `canvasBox`, `menuBox` and
`statusBox`: the 碎碎念 band was missing. So the real window began a full band (~154px, now 256px) ABOVE the origin the page
used for `canvas.style.top/left`, `placeMenu` and the per-pixel hit test (`paintedAt`, `isOverMenu`, `isOverStatus`): the
pet and the menu were PAINTED about a band higher than the page believed, the launcher was told "transparent pixel" over its
own visible pet and put the window into click-through — clicks fell through the pet, and the menu's rows were not where its
hit test looked for them.

Why it read as 「莫名奇妙」: a 状态 prompt IS in that union, so a prompt up cancelled most of the offset, and while the band was
the MEASURED bubble it was reported only while a remark was up. Since cf59a16 the band is reported unconditionally, which
would have made it permanent.

`plannedOrigin()` now unions the same reported band (`speechBand`), so the page's assumed origin IS the launcher's union.

VERIFIED, figures as printed: client-test 97 in a real browser, desktop **269 WITH the real window**, core 1120,
host+model 750, `client-chunks --check` 3/3 fresh. Counts in the docs do not move. NOT run: check-docs.mjs, verify-all.mjs.

symptoms; the click test on the real pet is the confirmation that matters.
